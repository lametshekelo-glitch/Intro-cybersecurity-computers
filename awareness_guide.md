CYBERSECURITY AWARENESS GUIDE FOR STUDENTS

1. INTRODUCTION
Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, mobile devices, networks and personal information from cyber threats.

2. COMMON CYBERSECURITY THREATS
- Phishing: Fake messages used to steal personal information.
- Malware: Harmful software that damages devices or steals data.
- Social engineering: Tricking people into sharing confidential information.

3. HOW STUDENTS CAN STAY SAFE ONLINE
- Use strong and unique passwords.
- Enable two-factor authentication.
- Avoid clicking suspicious links 

4. WHAT TO DO WHEN ATTACKED
- Stop interacting with suspicious messages.
- Change compromised passwords.
- Report the incident to IT support.
- Check your account activity.
- Contact your bank if financial information is exposed.

5. CONCLUSION
Cybersecurity awareness helps students protect their personal information, academic work and money.

Remember: Protect your data, protect your future, protect your money.
